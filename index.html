Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

1. Overview

Brent Opsomer is responsible for the personal data described in this Privacy Policy when you use the FIFTN app (“App”). For privacy questions or requests, use the contact details in section 11.

2. My Segments and Local Gameplay

Names, colours and texts you enter in My Segments are saved in the App’s local storage. FIFTN does not upload these entries to its servers or include their names or texts in the gameplay events it sends to PostHog. A gameplay event may record that a custom segment was used, without recording what was written in it. Player names entered for local gameplay are used in the game; you do not need to use real names.

You can delete saved segments in the App when the editor is available. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local saves. FIFTN does not provide its own cloud sync for My Segments. Device backups may copy local app data depending on your operating system and settings.

3. Data Used by Online Services

3.1 Firebase Authentication and Backend

FIFTN uses Google Firebase to assign an anonymous account identifier without asking you for an email address or password. Firebase services support game content, Premium access checks, and promo code redemption. The backend uses account and network information to limit abuse and records redemptions and the access they grant. For promotional rewards, it also records the claim time and access expiry against your Firebase account identifier to prevent repeat claims. These rewards do not create a subscription or renew automatically.

3.2 Purchases and Trials

Apple or Google processes payments. FIFTN uses RevenueCat to check and restore access and receives purchase metadata such as the product, trial status, and access expiry. FIFTN does not receive your full payment card details.

3.3 Product Analytics

Where enabled, PostHog receives app events such as game starts, wheel results by category, Premium screen views, and purchase and trial events, along with app and device information. Purchase analytics may include the product identifier, trial status and expiry. Promo redemption analytics may include a campaign label and an internal code reference, rather than the redeemable code itself. We use these events to understand gameplay and improve the App. Events can be associated with your Firebase account identifier. In app versions with the analytics choice, PostHog starts only after you allow optional analytics. You can change your choice in Settings > Privacy without affecting gameplay, Premium or purchases. Turning analytics off stops new analytics collection in the app; it does not automatically erase events already sent. Older app versions may have associated the Firebase identifier with RevenueCat analytics.

3.4 Advertising

The free version may display ads through Google AdMob. FIFTN requests non-personalized ads in its end-of-game ad flow. Ad services may still process device or network information and identifiers to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse, depending on the platform and your settings.

4. How We Use Data and Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

  • To provide the App and grant purchased or redeemed access, as necessary to provide the service you request.
  • To keep the App secure and prevent abuse, based on our legitimate interests where applicable.

  • For advertising, consent where required by applicable law.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

5. Recipients

The services described above involve Google Firebase, RevenueCat, PostHog and Google AdMob. Apple and Google also handle purchases through their own stores under their own privacy policies.
